Questions & answers

Got questions?

Straight answers to what writers ask us most.

01Do I need a finished script to work with you?

Not at all. You can work with us at every stage:

  • Just an idea? Use a Starter Kit workbook to develop your story from idea to script.
  • Finished an outline? Submit it for an Outline Review to see if it’s ready to move into pages.
  • Finished a draft? Submit it for Development Notes and get studio-level insight for your rewrite.
  • Need to talk it through? Book a Story Jam with a Story Expert.
02How do I know which service is right for me?

It depends on where your script is and the kind of support you want. If you’re not sure, book a free Kick-Off Jam: a quick 1:1 chat with a Story Expert who’ll point you to the right fit.

03Can you help if I’m just getting started?

Absolutely. Many writers come to us long before they have a draft. The Academy, our budget-friendly film school, lives inside our community and offers self-paced lessons, live courses and feedback from Story Experts.

01Do you offer script coverage?

No. Traditional coverage is written for executives, not writers. We offer Development Notes instead: clear, page-by-page guidance on story, structure and character, so you know exactly what to fix in your rewrite.

02What’s the difference between Development Notes and Story Jams?

Development Notes are written, page-by-page notes on a full draft. Perfect before you submit to competitions or pitch.

Story Jams are live, 1-hour sessions where we build out your story together, from concept to plot. Ideal if you have an idea, an outline, early pages or questions about your notes.

03How long do Development Notes take?

About 14 business days. Pay-later options are available, and scripts over the page limit are $4 per extra page.

01Do I need an agent or manager to work with you?

No. You don’t need representation or industry connections. We help you build strong material first; representation comes later.

02Can international writers work with you?

Yes. Writers from anywhere in the world can work with us. All notes, courses and sessions are delivered online.

03Do you sign NDAs? How do I protect my work?

We don’t sign NDAs. The best protection is to register your script or outline with the U.S. Copyright Office before sharing it. That gives you legal proof of ownership.
